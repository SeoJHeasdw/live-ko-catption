Local translation comparison fixtures

24 synthetic examples: 12 English→Korean and 12 Korean→English, with six general
and six IT cases in each direction. These are authored examples, not private user
transcripts or a representative quality benchmark. They do not test speech
recognition, live microphone delay, long-session thermals, or fan noise.

Schema
- direction and domain use CaptionCore enum raw values.
- previousSentence, when present, is exactly one short preceding source sentence.
  Give it to the local model as context, but request translation of source only.
  An Apple source-only baseline does not receive equivalent separate context;
  identify that difference in reports rather than attributing all gains to model
  size. If a context-inclusive Apple comparison is added, label it separately.
- goldenMeaning describes facts and relationships to check, not a reference
  string for exact matching. Equivalent natural translations are acceptable.
- requiredTerms are terminology review hints. Compare meaning and allowed
  equivalents, not raw case-sensitive substring presence. In these fixtures the
  fixed precision/recall classifier meanings are deliberate.
- expectedNumbers describes values, units, and times to preserve semantically.
  Localized forms such as 120밀리초, 120 ms, 오전 9시 30분, and 9:30 a.m. are valid.
- ambiguityNote explains the intended context choice and facts not to invent.

Review each output for meaning preservation, naturalness, domain terminology,
and added/omitted facts. Negation, conditional scope, self-corrections, denominator
definitions, units, dates, and event order matter more than surface wording.
Do not treat a fluent but false caption as an improvement. Do not mark ambiguity
as solved if the output simply copies the previous sentence into the caption.

Report actual warm/cold timing measurements separately from language quality.
Both a source-only local run and a context-enabled local run can be useful, so
the context benefit and model benefit are visible. No quality percentages or
latency claims are established by this fixture file alone.
